What helps us help you

If something isn't working, these make it much faster to sort out:

  • Your device and OS version — iPhone 14, iOS 18.2, that sort of thing.
  • What you were doing when it happened.
  • A screenshot, if there was something on screen.

You don't need to include anything you talked about in the app. We don't need it to fix a bug, and you shouldn't have to share it to get help.

Billing and subscriptions

Subscriptions are handled by the App Store and by Google Play, not by us, so cancellations and refunds go through them:

  • iPhone or iPad: Settings, your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Android: the Play Store app, your profile, then Payments and subscriptions.

Email us anyway if something looks wrong — we can't process the refund, but we can tell you what we see on our side.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, or from the account deletion page.

If you need help now

Inner Map is a general wellness app. It is not therapy, it is not treatment, and it is not an emergency service. If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please contact your local emergency number, or in the US call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. findahelpline.com lists services by country.
